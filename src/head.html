    The Cursed Board

    Race through the ruins and be first to claim the emerald at the heart of the temple.

    • Roll and walk that many tiles. At a fork, tap a glowing tile to choose your way.
    • Land on 3 different purple relic tiles to break the temple seal. Then you can step onto a temple door.
    • Reaching a temple door ends your move and rolls a random event: the door opens (straight to the emerald, you win) or stays shut (you continue around the inner ring).
    • Gold tiles draw a lucky card. Blue tiles ask a riddle. Red tiles are traps.
    • Land exactly on a diamond tile to trigger a random event. Orange diamonds are rope-bridge shortcuts and green diamonds are ways to go inside. Passing over one does nothing.
    • Going inside stays sealed until you have touched all four green diamonds on the outer ring.
    • Land on a rival to send them home. Reach the emerald to win.
